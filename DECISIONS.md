DP1 — What happens when a creator declines a booking?

Decision: The booking is marked Declined and remains visible in the client's booking history. The client can then return to the marketplace and book another available gig.
Reason: This preserves the booking history while giving the client a clear next action instead of deleting the request.

DP2 — Can a gig receive another booking while one is pending?


Decision: No. A gig can have only one active booking at a time.

Pending → unavailable for other bookings
Accepted → unavailable for other bookings
Declined → becomes available again

Reason: This prevents double-booking and avoids creators receiving multiple active requests for the same gig.


DP3 — How are gigs ranked/discovered?

Decision: Gigs are shown newest first by default. Clients can then use search, category filters, and price sorting to find relevant gigs.

Reason: New gigs get fair visibility while clients still have control over how they discover and compare services.
